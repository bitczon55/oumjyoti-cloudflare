About

Yeh platform bhakti, seva, manavta aur samajik sahyog jaise moolyon ko logon tak pahunchane ke uddeshya se banaya gaya hai. Hamara prayas hai ki samaj ke zaruratmand logon tak sahayata, seva aur sakaratmak sandesh pahunch sake.

Hamara vishwas hai ki sachchi bhakti keval pooja aur prarthana tak simit nahi hoti, balki zaruratmand vyakti ki madad karna, bhookhe ko bhojan dena, buzurgon ka samman karna, bachchon ki shiksha mein sahayata karna aur samaj mein daya aur karuna ka bhav badhana bhi bhakti ka ek mahatvapurn roop hai.

Is platform ke madhyam se seva se judi jaankari, samajik sahayata ke prayas aur manavta se sambandhit vicharon ko ek jagah par prastut karne ka prayas kiya ja raha hai. Hamara uddeshya logon ko ek doosre ki madad karne aur samaj ke prati apni zimmedari samajhne ke liye prerit karna hai.

Gau seva, ann sahayata, gareeb aur zaruratmand parivaron ki madad, buzurgon ki seva, bachchon ki shiksha mein sahayata, swasthya sambandhi sahayata aur prakritik aapda se prabhavit logon ki madad jaise seva karyon ko samajik bhavna ke saath aage badhane ka prayas kiya jayega.

Hamara maanna hai ki chhoti si seva bhi kisi vyakti ke jeevan mein bada badlav la sakti hai. Isi bhavna ke saath hum daya, karuna, satya, seva, sahyog aur manavta ke moolyon ko badhava dene ka prayas karte hain.

Yeh platform sabhi logon ko ek saath jodne, seva ke prati jagrukta badhane aur samaj mein sakaratmak bhavna ko majboot karne ki disha mein ek chhota sa prayas hai.

Services

Contact

Agar aap kisi seva karyakram mein sahyog karna chahte hain, samajik seva se judna chahte hain ya apne kshetra mein kisi zaruratmand vyakti ki sahayata ke liye jaankari dena chahte hain, to sampark ke madhyam se apni baat hum tak pahunchai ja sakti hai.

Hamara prayas rahega ki prapt sandeshon aur jaankari ko uchit roop se dekha jaye aur sambhav hone par zarurat ke anusaar sahayata ya margdarshan pradan kiya ja sake.
